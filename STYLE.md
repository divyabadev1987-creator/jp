# Reference editing style

Breakdown of the reference video (gaming facecam + gameplay, ~1:41). The reference video itself lives in `ref/`, which is not committed.

## 1. Facecam intro (~0–12 s)
- Full-screen camera, warm-lit room; punch-in zooms on the face for emphasis.
- Pop-up title text: large bold light-blue words with white + dark outline, tilted, placed behind the speaker.
- Word-by-word captions: pixel/retro game font, bottom centre, white with dark shadow; highlight words in lime green and purple; 1–2 words at a time with a quick glitch flicker between captions.

## 2. Transition
- ~1 s of black, then hard cut into gameplay.

## 3. Gameplay
- Full-screen 16:9 gameplay, game HUD left visible.
- Facecam box top-right, ~22% of frame width, no border.
- Long uncut runs (~10 cuts per 100 s).

## 4. Audio
- Commentary on top, game audio under it.
- Reference measures -20.5 LUFS; target -14 LUFS for YouTube.
